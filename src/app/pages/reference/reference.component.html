Wire Type

Use automotive stranded copper wire rated for engine-bay/automotive temperatures. Avoid household THHN, extension-cord wire, random appliance wire, or speaker wire for power circuits. Use proper automotive terminals, heat-shrink, loom, grommets, and secure mounting.

Critical Fuse Principle

Don't assume a fixed rule such as "12 AWG always = 20A" or "10 AWG always = 30A." Safe fuse selection depends on actual wire ampacity, wire length, voltage drop, temperature, bundling, installation location, expected continuous load, and manufacturer specifications. The fuse protects the wire and circuit — when a manufacturer specifies a fuse size, follow it unless the overall design requires a documented change.
